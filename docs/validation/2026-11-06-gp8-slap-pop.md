# GP8 parity: bass slap and pop sounds (T22, T23)

Date: 2026-11-06. Branch `feature/t22-slap-pop` from `develop` at `c56ac1b`.

## What changed

A note on a beat marked slap or pop now plays its own recorded sound on both bass voices. Before, the mark only removed the track's playing style and the note played the ordinary fingered voice.

- The recordings are "BMC slap bass samples" by Bruce H. McCosar, used under CC BY 3.0 (https://ccmixter.org/files/bmccosar/13742). No NC-licensed material is used.
- `assets/audio/sources.json` adds the source `slap` with `kind: "archive"`. Preparation downloads the Internet Archive copy of the ccMixter zip (`web.archive.org/web/20201020042708id_/…`; ccMixter refuses direct automated downloads). It checks the size (896,296 bytes) and SHA-256 (`6b2af354…610d53`, stored as the source `revision`) before use.
- Extraction takes only flat entry names, at most 64 entries, 16 MiB each and 64 MiB in total. Every used file is then checked against `slap-inventory.json`.
- App-owned maps (`assets/audio/slap/maps/slap.sfz`, `pop.sfz`) and the attribution and license file (`assets/audio/slap/LICENSE.txt`) are copied into the cache on every run and pinned like any other input. Their bytes are part of the preparation fingerprint. They are marked `-text`, so the pinned bytes are the same on every OS.
- The instruments carry `extends: true`, so their regions join the existing `darkblack-bass` and `babyblue-bass` voices. Each voice gains 6 slap and 6 pop regions.
- FLAC recordings ship unchanged. Preparation reads their frame count from STREAMINFO, and the native loader decodes them with Symphonia.
- The native loader accepts `CC-BY-3.0` beside `CC0-1.0`, and a 64-digit hex revision (archive SHA-256) beside a 40-digit commit. It rejects any other licence and any non-hex revision.
- `slap` and `pop` are new articulations in TypeScript and Rust. In `plan.ts` they rank after dead, palm mute and ghost, and before staccato and legato. A tapped note or a harmonic on a slap or pop beat keeps its tapped or harmonic voice, since the recordings are ordinary pitches. An instrument without slap or pop layers (guitars) falls back to its ordinary voice, with the bright picked filter.
- Preparation fetches the archive once per run, with a 2-minute timeout, and writes it atomically. A cached archive that fails its hash is downloaded again when online. Entries always come from the verified archive, never from loose cached copies. The packaged notice copies the pinned `slap/LICENSE.txt`.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `assets/audio/README.md` carry the attribution. The license and inventory are packaged under `licenses/audio/slap/` by the existing `copy-notices.mjs` loop.

## Mapping and tuning

Slap roots: A1 33, Bb1 34, B1 35, C2 36 (also plays 37), D2 38, Eb2 39. Pop roots are an octave higher, 45–51. Notes below and above each set use its end sample, transposed. The pack has no C#. Its short, hammer-on and thump recordings are not used.

Settled pitch, measured by autocorrelation 0.45 s into each bundled recording (the ignored test `bundled_slap_and_pop_recordings_sound_at_their_root_keys`):

| Slap | Measured | Pop | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| A_long | 32.93 | apop_long | 45.17 |
| Bb_long | 34.02 | bbpop_long | 46.06 |
| B_long | 35.03 | bpop_long | 47.20 |
| C_long | 36.06 | cpop_long | 48.11 |
| D_long | 38.02 | dpop_long | 50.08 |
| Eb_long | 39.00 | ebpop_long | 51.09 |

All are within 20 cents of their mapped key, so no tuning offset is applied. Measured over the first 250 ms, the attacks read 20–40 cents sharp; that is the natural pitch glide of a hard-struck string.

## Levels

At velocity 110, the raw recordings measured 10–15 dB below the fingered layers in RMS over the first 0.5 s, with a higher crest factor. The map gains match them partway, so their peaks stay near the fingered peaks: darkblack slap +10 dB and pop +11 dB, babyblue slap +6 dB and pop +7 dB. After the gains, darkblack slap A1 measures −13.3 dB RMS against −9.6 dB fingered; its peaks are +4.4 dB and +0.6 dB. The user listened to the demo on 2026-11-06 and approved these levels by ear. A 16-bit demo is written to `.analysis/slap-pop-demo.wav`. It has fingered, slap and pop pairs, then low slaps and high pops, on darkblack and then babyblue. It is generated output and is not committed.

## Review

A fresh read-only reviewer found no high-severity issues. Two medium findings were fixed: parallel sample workers could download the archive up to 8 times and write it non-atomically, and slap outranked harmonics and taps. Low findings fixed: the stale extracted cache, the missing timeout and the encoded `content-length` check, slap/pop in the CI resource-budget test, and the unpinned notice copy. The reviewer confirmed that fflate's inflate output is bounded by the declared size, that path confinement holds, and that the offline path still catches edits. Still open: no test drives the `extends` merge or the download path directly.

## Limits

- The levels were approved by ear from the demo. The guitar fallback's brightness is still only a measured choice, and no slap score was heard in the desktop app.
- One velocity layer and no round robin: every slap at a key is the same recording. The short recordings could later give staccato slaps.
- Wide transposition at the ends: a G3 pop plays Eb3 shifted up 4 semitones, and an E1 slap plays A1 shifted down 5.
- Golpe still has no sound of its own; dead slap keeps the dead-note voice. T23 stays partial.
- No automatic slap/pop track style (S22): a track's style is still pick or fingers.
- Setup and CI now depend on web.archive.org serving the pinned zip. A changed or missing copy fails closed on the hash check.
- No desktop playback run of a slap score was made in this slice.
