# GP8 parity A13: wah and auto-wah (2026-10-23)

GP8's effect pedals include a wah and an auto-wah. This slice adds both to TabPlayer as a native Wah block on every track's sound, editable in the Sound task. It was implemented on branch `feat/gp8-parity`.

## Behavior
- **Placement:** the Wah block sits before the amp, as a pedal would: Gate, Compressor, **Wah**, Amp, Cabinet, EQ and the later blocks. It can be turned on and off, and each control change is one recorded, undoable sound edit.
- **Filter:**
  - It is a resonant band-pass that passes its centre at unity and cuts frequencies away from it.
  - The centre runs from 350 Hz at the heel to 2.2 kHz at the toe, spaced evenly in pitch.
  - Resonance (0–1) narrows the peak.
  - Mix (0–1) blends the dry signal back in.
  - The centre is the same at any sample rate.
- **Manual mode:** Pedal position (0 = heel, 1 = toe) sets the centre. Sensitivity is disabled in this mode.
- **Auto mode:**
  - The playing level moves the pedal. The envelope rises in 2 ms and falls over 200 ms, so each note opens the filter and it closes as the note decays.
  - Sensitivity (0–1) sets how loud the playing must be. At the default, the heel is at −50 dBFS and the toe is 36 dB louder. A loud pickup, near −20 dBFS here, opens the filter most of the way.
  - Pedal position is disabled in this mode.
- **Smooth changes:**
  - The pedal glides with a 15 ms time constant, so position edits and mode changes sweep instead of jumping.
  - Switching the block on or off crossfades over 10 ms.
  - Switching it on starts from rest, not from the level and pedal it had when it was switched off.
  - Full resonance does not lift a full-scale tone, and silence decays to exact zero.
- **Compatibility and validation:**
  - Sounds saved before the wah load with it switched off at its defaults.
  - A wah left at its defaults is not written, so older documents keep the same saved form.
  - The native engine and the editor both reject an unknown mode, and the native engine also rejects values out of range.
- **Output:** the wah applies to live playback, WAV and stem export, and practice-speed renders.

## Evidence
- **Native** (`src-tauri/tests/audio_wah.rs`, 6 tests):
  - **Format and validation:**
    - the wire format, and loading an older document;
    - every range and mode check;
    - exact bypass while the block is off.
  - **Manual sweep:**
    - the loudest frequency is 300–420 Hz at the heel, 750–1,000 Hz in the middle and 1.9–2.5 kHz at the toe;
    - the peak gain is 0.85–1.1, and the gain two octaves up is below 0.35;
    - more resonance narrows the peak, and mix 0 is dry;
    - the centre matches within 7% at 44.1 kHz.
  - **Auto:**
    - a loud 2.1 kHz tone passes above 0.6, and a soft one below 0.2;
    - soft playing favours 400 Hz;
    - lower sensitivity needs louder playing;
    - a −20 dBFS tone centres at 1–2 kHz, not pinned at the toe;
    - the filter closes again after loud playing stops;
    - switched off and on again, it starts closed. A mutation check showed this test fails when that reset is removed.
  - **Clicks and level:**
    - heel-to-toe and toe-to-heel edits and a manual-to-auto switch with a large pedal move stay within 1.6× a steady tone's largest step;
    - full resonance peaks below 1.1 at the heel, middle and toe;
    - silence after a burst reaches exact zero.
- **Editor** (`src/audio/wah-effect.test.tsx`):
  - the defaults;
  - completing older sounds, and rejecting an unknown mode;
  - the block order;
  - each control's recorded edit;
  - the mode-dependent disabled controls.

  `src/audio/modulation-effects.test.tsx` covers the full block order.
- **Desktop:** `tests/desktop/parity-wah-effect.mjs` on a Windows desktop-test build exported WAV files through the real Export dialog. Brightness is the RMS of the first difference over the RMS of the signal.

  | Render | Brightness |
  |---|---|
  | dry | 0.104 |
  | heel | 0.069 |
  | toe | 0.214 |
  | auto | 0.141, between heel and toe |

  The panel recorded the pedal and mode edits. Live Play produced native output with no error. Undo restored manual mode with the pedal at the toe. There were no page errors.

  `parity-modulation-effects.mjs` and `exports.mjs` still pass on the same build.
- **Verification:**
  - every Rust test target except `file_library_intake`, which has a compile error that predates this slice;
  - the full `vitest` suite: 3,438 tests, with the load-sensitive `tuning-ui`, `corpus` and `WorkspaceApp` files passing when run alone;
  - `tsc`.
- **Review:** an adversarial review found no filter or state-handling defect. It reported these issues, all fixed:
  - auto mode was fully open above −35 dBFS, so it acted as a fixed toe filter. It now maps the level in dB, calibrated to the app's track levels;
  - the mode-switch no-click test never moved the pedal;
  - switching the block on reused a stale envelope and pedal;
  - inactive controls were always enabled;
  - the desktop test did not show auto mode following the playing;
  - constant coefficients were computed every sample.

  It also found that the previous commit had put the modulation effect types outside `FxSettings` in `src/edit-types.ts`. That was fixed in its own commit, e1ea166, and checked with `tsc` on the committed tree.

  The fixes were checked by the tests above, not by a second review.

## Limits
- A13 is partial:
  - **no automation:** the pedal position cannot be automated over the score, so a pedal cannot be rocked through a passage;
  - **one model:** it is one generic wah, not GP8's specific pedal models or presets;
  - **auto mode:** it follows level only, with fixed attack and release times;
  - **older builds:** a wah that was edited but left off is written to the saved document. An older TabPlayer build rejects that document's sound;
  - **not run:** `native-audio.mjs` was not run, because it reloads into session recovery and fails before this slice;
  - **no reference:** there is no comparison with original GP8 audio.
